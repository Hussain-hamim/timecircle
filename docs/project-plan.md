# Social Discovery Map

## The Idea

We’re building a **social discovery platform for discovering places, people, and things happening around you**.

Think **TikTok/Instagram + Google Maps + Nomadtable**, but focused on real-world experiences rather than just directions or travel.

Users can discover interesting cafés, restaurants, events, hidden spots, activities, communities, and hangout locations through short-form posts, photos, and videos. Instead of simply searching for a place on a map, users discover it through **what other people are actually doing there**.

The map becomes a living social layer of the city.

## How It Works

Users open the app and can:

* Explore a map of interesting places and activities nearby
* See short videos/photos from people at those locations
* Discover hidden or trending spots
* Create posts around places they visit
* Tag locations, activities, interests, and vibes
* Create or join activities/meetups
* See people who are interested in the same activities
* Create group plans and chat with participants
* Save places and build personal collections
* Get AI-powered recommendations based on their interests and behavior

For example:

**“I’m bored tonight.”**

Instead of giving the user a list of Google Maps results, the app could show:

> ☕ Cozy café — people hanging out here tonight
> 🎸 Live music — 8 people interested
> 🌅 Sunset spot — trending nearby
> 🍜 New ramen place — popular with people who like your interests

The user can then discover the place, see the social content around it, and potentially join or create a plan with other people.

## The Nomadtable Influence

We take the strongest parts of the Nomadtable experience:

* Nearby people
* Activities and meetups
* Create/join plans
* Future plans
* Group conversations
* AI recommendations
* Interest-based matching

But we combine them with a much larger **place/content discovery layer**.

Nomadtable is primarily about **meeting people while traveling**. Our idea can work for **travelers, locals, students, friends, and anyone looking for something to do**.

So the core loop becomes:

**Discover → See what's happening → Find people → Make a plan → Go there → Post your experience → Discover something else**

## The Social Feed + Map

The two main experiences are:

### 1. Map

A visual map showing:

* Interesting places
* Trending spots
* Events
* Activities
* User-created plans
* Social hotspots

### 2. Social Feed

A TikTok-style discovery feed containing:

* Short videos
* Photos
* Place recommendations
* “Things to do”
* Local discoveries
* Events
* User experiences

Every piece of content can connect back to a real-world location.

So instead of:

**“Here is a video.”**

It becomes:

**“Here is a video → this is where it is → here are other people interested → here's what you can do there.”**

## AI Layer

AI can make the discovery experience personalized.

It can automatically understand posts and tag:

* Location
* Type of place
* Activity
* Cuisine
* Atmosphere/vibe
* Price level
* Time of day
* Interests
* Event type

Then the recommendation engine learns what each user likes.

Eventually, users could simply ask:

**“What should I do tonight?”**

or

**“Find me a chill place where people my age are hanging out.”**

and the app builds a personalized set of places, activities, and social options.

The goal is to become the **social layer of the real world**.

Google Maps tells you **where a place is**.
